Enquiry Desk

A contact form that actually does something with what people send. Each message is checked, saved as a row in your Google Sheet, answered with a confirmation email straight away, and sorted so you know what it's about before you open it.

Sort with Claude instead of keyword rules

Paste an Anthropic key and each message is read by Claude, which picks a category, an urgency and writes a one-line summary. If Claude's answer is malformed or slow, the keyword rules take over, so nothing is lost. Your key stays in this tab and goes only to api.anthropic.com.

    How it works
    
    
    Most contact forms just forward an email, and messages get lost or never answered. Enquiry Desk is the part behind the form. When someone presses Send, it first checks the message is real and quietly drops spam bots. Then it saves the message as a new row in your Google Sheet, so nothing is ever lost. It emails the sender straight away to say their enquiry was received, with a reference number, and emails you a short summary. Claude can read each message and mark whether it's a job offer, a project or a question, and how urgent it is.
